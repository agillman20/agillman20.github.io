Senior Design Project: Wi-Fly
======

<div class="project">
<figure class="project-figure"><img src="/images/undergrad/wi-fly.jpg" alt="Screenshot of the Wi-Fly app showing Wi-Fi coverage over a floor plan"></figure>

<p><strong>Team:</strong> Alexander Balkum, Shurui Chen, Fortino Garcia, Sarah Schwettmann</p>

<p><strong>Title:</strong> Faster and More Accurate App for Optimal Router Placement</p>

<p><strong>Abstract:</strong> The team created Wi-Fly: a fast and accurate app for optimal selection of Wi-Fi router location. On a mobile platform, users can input their home or office floor plan into the app, and select a potential router location. The application calculates and superimposes the solution to the Helmholtz equation on the geometry, allowing users to visualize the Wi-Fi coverage of their space. In this manner, users can select the router location that meets their coverage needs before physically moving the router. By using Boundary Integral Equation methods to solve the wave propagation problem, the application is accurate and more user-friendly than others on the market.</p>

<p><strong>Directions for use:</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li>In one motion, draw your floor plan on screen.</li>
  <li>If geometry is displayed on screen, press the check mark on the top of screen.</li>
  <li>Place your Wi-Fi router anywhere in the enclosed region and a few seconds later the (scattered field) coverage will appear.</li>
  <li>Change placement of the router by pressing another location on the screen. To change the geometry, press on the trash can and start at step 1.</li>
</ol>
